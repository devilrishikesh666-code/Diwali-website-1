The Festival of Lights

A story as old as light itself

Diwali celebrates the triumph of light over darkness and good over evil. Homes are cleaned, lamps are lit, and hearts are opened to family and friends.

From villages across India to cities around the world, families share sweets, prayers, and laughter. Every flame carries one simple hope: a brighter year ahead.
